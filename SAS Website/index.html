Who are we?

Space of Arts Students (SAS) is a not-for-profit, student-led club, dedicated to creating a vibrant community at Nosham within the Arts and beyond!

From unforgettable social events to supporting students in developing industry connections, SAS is designed to help you get the most out of your university experience.
