# SAS-Website

Website URL: https://space-of-art-student-nosham.netlify.app/

The Assignment 3 homepage website is designed to introduce the student-led arts club
“Space of Arts Student Nosham”
, promote arts-related events and publications, and
encourage students to join the community. The target audience is mainly the undergraduate
students aged around 18-26, especially the Arts first-year students who are looking for social
connection, academic support or career opportunities.
